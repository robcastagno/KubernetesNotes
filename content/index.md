I take notes as I work on personal projects. Some of those notes might help others who want to try out similar projects to me. I mostly share these to be looked at alongside my description of the projects located on my [Website](https://www.robertcastagno.com), it's not recommended to use these notes on their own without the context, as there's very little explanation about what is actually going on.

Documenting your process is extremely valuable to learning how to use a new thing, especially when it comes to bugfixing and repeating previous actions.
